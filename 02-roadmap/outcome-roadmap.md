# Outcome Roadmap & Trade-off Memo: Fable Growth

> Module 2 · Turn Your Strategy into an Outcome Roadmap

## 1. Trade-off memo

> **Rock 1:** Add a daily streak feature to drive habit formation (supports KR2): Encourages repeat engagement and habit formation through visible progress and positive reinforcement. Could increase the likelihood of customers returning after their acute needs have subsided.
>
> **Rock 2:** Add AI-generated daily check-in prompts personalized to user history (supports KR1): Uses customer history and preferences to deliver relevant well being reflections, helping users recognise progress and discover continued value beyond acute stress relief.
>
> **Rock 3:** Build a content library: guided meditations, articles, audio (supports KR3): Additional guided meditations, articles and audio could strengthen the premium value proposition by providing variety and supporting changing well being needs.
>
> **Hard no 1:** Redesign the onboarding flow based on 40% drop-off data: Fable's primary constraint is retention, not initial activation.
>
> **Hard no 2:** Localize the app into Spanish and Portuguese: Localisation expands reach without resolving the underlying retention problem.
>
> **Hard no 3:** Introduce “Fable for Teams”, a B2B workplace wellbeing offer: It introduces a second business model before Fable has solved the sustainability of its first.
